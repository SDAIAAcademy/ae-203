# P01 · Fares and zones

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P01.1 Single fares

A single journey costs SAR 4 within one zone and SAR 6 across two or more zones. One fare covers transfers between metro and bus within 90 minutes of the first tap.

## P01.2 Zones

The network has three zones. Central, Old Souq and University stations are in zone 1; Airport and Industrial are in zone 3.

## P01.3 Paying

Riders pay with a Darb card, a contactless bank card or the Darb app. Cash is accepted only at ticket machines, not on buses.
