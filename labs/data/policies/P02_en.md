# P02 · Daily and weekly fare caps

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P02.1 Daily cap

Card and app payments stop charging after SAR 16 in one service day. The cap applies per card, not per person.

## P02.2 Weekly cap

From Sunday to Saturday, charges stop after SAR 70 per card.

## P02.3 Overcharges

A rider charged above the cap can ask for the difference through a fare-charge ticket within 30 days.
