# P03 · Refunds for delays

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P03.1 When a refund is due

A metro journey delayed by 15 minutes or more, or a bus journey delayed by 25 minutes or more, earns a refund of the fare paid for that journey.

## P03.2 How to claim

Claims are made within 14 days of the journey with the card or app used. The refund is credited to the same card or app within 5 working days.

## P03.3 Exclusions

No refund is due for delays announced at least 24 hours in advance, such as planned maintenance.
