# P04 · Refunds for cancelled trips

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P04.1 Cancelled trips

When a trip is cancelled and the next trip on the same route leaves more than 30 minutes later, the rider's fare for that journey is refunded in full.

## P04.2 Taxi costs

Darb does not refund taxi or ride-hailing costs.
