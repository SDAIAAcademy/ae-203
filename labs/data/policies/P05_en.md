# P05 · Concession fares

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P05.1 Students

Registered students pay 50 percent of the single fare with a personalised student card.

## P05.2 Seniors

Riders aged 60 and over travel free with a personalised senior card.

## P05.3 Children

Children under 6 travel free with a paying adult; up to two children per adult.
