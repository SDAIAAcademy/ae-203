# P06 · Riders with disabilities

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P06.1 Free travel

Riders with a disability card and one companion travel free on metro and bus.

## P06.2 Step-free access

Every metro station has lifts. All buses have a ramp and two wheelchair spaces.

## P06.3 Assistance booking

Station assistance can be booked through the app or the contact centre at least 2 hours before travel.
