# P07 · Lost property

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P07.1 Reporting

Report a lost item through a lost-property ticket with the route, the date and a description of the item.

## P07.2 Keeping items

Found items are kept at the Central station office for 30 days, then donated or destroyed.

## P07.3 Collecting

Collect an item in person with an identity document; a collection fee of SAR 10 applies to items kept more than 7 days.
