# P09 · Bicycles and scooters

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P09.1 Bicycles

Folded bicycles are allowed at all times. Full-size bicycles are allowed on the metro outside peak periods only.

## P09.2 E-scooters

E-scooters must be folded and switched off. Riding them in stations is not allowed.
