# P12 · Fare inspection and penalties

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P12.1 Inspection

Inspectors check that every rider has tapped in. The card or device used must be shown on request.

## P12.2 Penalty fare

A rider without a valid tap pays a penalty fare of SAR 200, reduced to SAR 100 if paid within 7 days.

## P12.3 Appeals

Appeals are made within 21 days through a penalty-appeal ticket.
