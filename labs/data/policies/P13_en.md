# P13 · The Darb card

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P13.1 Getting a card

A Darb card costs SAR 10 at ticket machines and station offices. The card itself does not expire.

## P13.2 Topping up

Cards can be topped up with SAR 10 to SAR 500 at machines or in the app.

## P13.3 Lost card

A registered card that is lost can be blocked in the app; its balance moves to a replacement card for SAR 10.
