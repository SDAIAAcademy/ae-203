# P14 · Service hours

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P14.1 Weekdays

Metro and buses run for 19 hours a day from Sunday to Thursday.

## P14.2 Weekends

On Friday the metro starts 4 hours later than on weekdays. On Saturday it runs the weekday timetable.

## P14.3 Ramadan

During Ramadan, service is extended by 2 hours after the usual last trip.
