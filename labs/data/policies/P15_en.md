# P15 · Complaints

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P15.1 How to complain

Complaints can be made through the app, the website, the rider assistant or the contact centre.

## P15.2 Response times

Darb acknowledges a complaint within 1 working day and answers within 10 working days.

## P15.3 Escalation

A rider who is not satisfied can ask for a review by the rider experience manager.
