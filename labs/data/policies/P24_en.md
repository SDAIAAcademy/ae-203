# P24 · Privacy notice

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P24.1 What Darb collects

Darb records card taps, ticket details and messages to the rider assistant to run the service and answer requests.

## P24.2 Retention

Messages to the rider assistant are kept for 90 days, with personal data masked.

## P24.3 Rights

Riders may ask to see or correct their data under the Personal Data Protection Law.
