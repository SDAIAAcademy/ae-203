# P25 · The rider assistant

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P25.1 What it is

Wasil is an AI assistant. It answers from Darb's published policies and live trip status, and it can open tickets.

## P25.2 What it does not do

Wasil does not approve refunds or cancel penalties; a staff member decides every refund.

## P25.3 Talking to a person

Riders can ask for a person at any time; the contact centre answers within 5 minutes on average.
