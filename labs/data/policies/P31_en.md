# P31 · Refunds for delays (2025 edition, superseded by P03)

Status: superseded by P03. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P31.1 When a refund was due

A metro journey delayed by 30 minutes or more earned a refund of the fare paid.

## P31.2 How to claim

Claims were made within 7 days of the journey.
