# P32 · Metro Red Line (M1) service pattern

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P32.1 Route

The Red Line runs between Al-Nakheel and Corniche with 6 stations: Al-Nakheel, University, King Road, Central, Old Souq, Corniche.

## P32.2 Frequency

Trains run every 4 minutes at peak and every 8 minutes off-peak.

## P32.3 End to end

A trip from end to end takes 20 minutes.
