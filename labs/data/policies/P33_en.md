# P33 · Metro Green Line (M2) service pattern

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P33.1 Route

The Green Line runs between Airport and Stadium with 6 stations: Airport, Exhibition, Central, Hospital, Al-Rawdah, Stadium.

## P33.2 Frequency

Trains run every 5 minutes at peak and every 10 minutes off-peak.

## P33.3 End to end

A trip from end to end takes 20 minutes.
