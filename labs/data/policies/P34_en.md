# P34 · Metro Blue Line (M3) service pattern

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P34.1 Route

The Blue Line runs between Industrial and Al-Yasmin with 6 stations: Industrial, Rail Station, Central, Al-Murooj, Wadi Park, Al-Yasmin.

## P34.2 Frequency

Trains run every 6 minutes at peak and every 12 minutes off-peak.

## P34.3 End to end

A trip from end to end takes 20 minutes.
