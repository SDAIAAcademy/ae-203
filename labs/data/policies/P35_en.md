# P35 · Metro Orange Line (M4) service pattern

Status: in force. Language: English. Edition: 2026.

## P35.1 Route

The Orange Line runs between Harbour and Hills with 6 stations: Harbour, Fish Market, Old Souq, Museum, Al-Rawdah, Hills.

## P35.2 Frequency

Trains run every 6 minutes at peak and every 12 minutes off-peak.

## P35.3 End to end

A trip from end to end takes 20 minutes.
